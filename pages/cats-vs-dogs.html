Cats vs dogs

A fair, light-hearted comparison of living with a cat and living with a dog.

The old debate, settled with a smile. Every animal is an individual, so treat these as tendencies, not rules.

Company

Dogs usually love a crowd and will happily follow you from room to room. Cats are often more independent, and decide for themselves when it is cuddle time. A Siamese, mind you, may follow you around and tell you all about it.

Exercise

Dogs generally need regular walks, whatever the weather. Cats usually exercise themselves with a good chase, a climb and a nap in the sun.

Training

Dogs are famously eager to learn commands. Cats can learn too, but usually only when they see a good reason, such as a treat.

Communication

A wagging tail usually means a happy dog. A cat's tail and ears tell a subtler story, and purring can mean contentment, though cats sometimes purr for other reasons too.

The verdict

There isn't one. The best pet is the one that suits your home, your time and your family. Many households happily have both.
